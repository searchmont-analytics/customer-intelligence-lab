## Capture checklist
1. **Published benchmark:** Module 1 (#module-1). Use the historical public scorecard, not the synthetic survey rates. Cite OLG Annual Report 2024–25 in the shot and retain the link in description.
2. **Research rigour:** Module 2 (#module-2), default 80% sample share, 40% target, 600 responses. Do not imply calibration can remove all bias.
3. **Segmentation:** Module 3 (#module-3), k=3; show the actual PCA and explain overlapping clusters. Do not describe them as real OLG segments.
4. **Voice of Customer:** Module 4 (#module-4), threshold 0.50. Show the actual model gate and manual coding interface. Generated evaluation labels are not professional human annotations.
5. **Portfolio:** Module 5 (#module-5), reset defaults, then select protection and apply recommendation. Do not imply measured financial ROI or actual corporate budget.
6. **Leadership:** Module 6 (#module-6). Capture the 90-day roadmap, quality gate and coaching planner. Label all profiles and goals proposed/illustrative.
7. **Landing page:** #home. Include a readable on-screen URL and avoid intrusive pointer movement.

## Presenter recording setup
- Three clips, horizontal 16:9, eye-level camera, preferably 4K or 1080p at 30 fps. Frame mid-chest to slightly above head, with consistent headroom; neutral professional background and soft frontal key light. Record in a quiet room; an external microphone is preferable to distant room audio.
- For a natural executive delivery: look into the lens, speak conversationally, pause after key sentences, avoid rushed delivery and excessive gestures. Leave five seconds before and after each take if possible.
- Record two takes of each segment, and choose the more natural one. Do not use generated likeness or cloned voice without explicit consent. Real recorded presenter segments take precedence over avatar substitutes.

## Audio, accessibility, delivery
- Use original or properly licensed music and sound effects; instrumental only. Keep narration intelligible over the score.
- Balance dialogue consistently (working stereo master target around −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP), then check actual audio and distribution needs.
- Create captions from the final timed recording and verify names, percentages and financial amounts. Add a caption file and accessible transcript; avoid tiny labels and colour-only interpretation.
- Final deliverables: MP4 (H.264/AAC, 1080p), caption SRT, thumbnail, description, and archived edit sources. Export only after review of the final cut and confirmed assets.

## Suggested YouTube description
Beyond the Numbers is an independent portfolio demonstration exploring how customer research, statistical analysis, evidence integration, strategic investment and people leadership can support better customer experience decisions. It uses historical publicly reported OLG benchmarks, fictional customer data and illustrative management scenarios. It does not represent OLG or contain proprietary customer records.

Explore the demonstration: https://searchmont-analytics.github.io/customer-intelligence-lab/

Source for historical metrics: OLG Annual Reports, fiscal 2023–24 and 2024–25 (see the website's public source register).
